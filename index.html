03 / Projects

Curiosity, put to work

Live Personal project · AI & full-stack

Job Pilot

An AI-powered job application platform I designed and built end to end. A reusable "Fact Library" of career achievements powers a tailored resume and cover letter for each job description, with a live in-browser document editor and templates matched to real hiring conventions in the US, UK, India, Germany, the Netherlands, Canada, Australia, and Europass.

Each account brings its own LLM key and can use Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Groq, or any LiteLLM-compatible provider. Built on FastAPI and PostgreSQL with pgvector for fact retrieval, deployed with Docker.
